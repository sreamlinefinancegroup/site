To make your ex miss you, stop all contact for a period, become visibly calmer and happier, let them notice what they lost without you explaining it, and keep your energy focused on yourself rather than on chasing them. People miss what is absent and valuable. Chasing makes you present and less valuable in their eyes; distance and self-respect do the opposite.

How to Get Your Ex Back: Psychology Tricks That Actually Help

  • Scarcity. We want what we cannot easily have. When you stop being available, your ex starts to notice the space you left.
  • Positive memory. The mind softens memories over time. Without new arguments, your ex begins to remember the good parts first.
  • Curiosity. When your ex cannot see everything you are doing, they wonder. Curiosity is the first step towards contact.
  • Social proof. Seeing you calm, busy and respected by others raises your value in their eyes.
  • Relief from pressure. People move towards what feels safe and light, not what feels like a demand.

The No Contact Rule — How to Use It

The no contact rule means no calls, texts, messages, likes or messages through friends for a set time — usually 30 days, sometimes up to 60. It is not a game or a punishment. It gives both of you time for emotions to cool, gives your ex space to feel your absence, and gives you time to heal and regain your strength.

  • Do not check their profiles constantly; it keeps you stuck.
  • If you share children or work, keep contact short, polite and practical.
  • If they reach out during no contact, reply warmly but briefly. Do not pour out your feelings.

How to Make an Avoidant Ex Miss You

An avoidant ex pulls away when things feel too close and starts missing you once the pressure is gone. Chasing an avoidant partner confirms their fear of being trapped. To make an avoidant ex miss you:

  • Give real space — longer no contact than with other exes.
  • When contact returns, keep it light, friendly and short.
  • Never demand a talk about "us" early on; let them move closer at their own pace.
  • Show that you are fine and independent. For an avoidant person, that feels safe.

How to Get Your Ex Back When They Say They Lost Feelings

When an ex says they "lost feelings", it usually means the good feelings were buried by stress, routine or resentment. Arguing with them about it only adds more resentment. Instead, accept what they say calmly, step back, and let time and distance do their work. When you reconnect, create new, light, positive moments rather than revisiting the breakup. New good feelings grow on top of old ones.

How to Get Your Ex to Notice You Again Without Talking

  • Share your real life on social media — not staged posts aimed at them, but genuine moments of growth, friends and new interests.
  • Look after your appearance and health. Change is noticed.
  • Stay kind with mutual friends; what they say about you reaches your ex.
  • Avoid posting sad quotes or indirect messages. They signal that you are still waiting.

How to Make Him Chase You After a Breakup

Men often chase what feels like a challenge and a prize. To make him chase you after a breakup, stop being the one who reaches out, let him wait a little for replies, and show that your life is full without him. When he does reach out, be warm and a little mysterious. Say yes to meeting only when you feel calm and ready — and let him plan it.

How to Get Your Ex Girlfriend to Regret Leaving

Women often decide to leave long before they actually go, so an ex girlfriend may seem finished with the relationship. To make her regret leaving, show her in real ways that the problems she left over have changed: that you listen, that you are steady, that you take care of yourself and others. Do not tell her you have changed — let her see it, through mutual friends or when you meet again.

How to Shift Your Energy to Make Him Obsessed

Energy is felt before it is explained. When you are desperate, anxious and focused on your ex, that energy pushes them away. When you are calm, confident and focused on your own life, it pulls them back. To shift your energy:

  • Manifest from fullness, not lack. Picture your ex returning to someone happy and whole — you — not to someone waiting in pain.
  • Use a daily visualisation. For five minutes each morning, see your ex messaging you, smiling, holding you. Feel it as already happening.
  • Write it as done. Each night write one sentence in the present tense: "Daniel and I are back together and happy."
  • Do self-love rituals. A cleansing bath, a rose quartz by your bed, time for yourself. Loving yourself changes how you are seen.

What to Say When Your Ex Reaches Out

When your ex finally messages, your first reply matters. Keep it warm, light and short: "Hey, nice to hear from you — how have you been?" Do not bring up the breakup, do not ask where you stand, and do not send long messages. Let them do a little more of the work. The aim of the first few conversations is simply to make talking to you feel good and easy again. This is how to sound irresistible to your ex: relaxed, kind and not in a hurry.

Mistakes That Push an Ex Further Away

  • Begging, crying or sending long emotional messages.
  • Showing up uninvited at their home or work.
  • Threatening, insulting or making them jealous on purpose.
  • Getting their friends and family to pressure them.
  • Jumping into a rebound relationship to prove a point.
