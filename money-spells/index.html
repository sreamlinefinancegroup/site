What Is a Money Spell?

A money spell is a ritual cast to attract wealth, open new opportunities and remove the spiritual blockages that keep money from reaching you or staying with you. Dr Sheikh Omar Rashid casts money spells for individuals and business owners who work hard but see little result, who lose money as fast as it comes, or who feel that something is working against them.

Money moves like energy. When the way is open, opportunities appear, customers arrive and payments come on time. When it is blocked — by bad luck, jealousy, a curse or simply negative energy — every effort seems to fail. A money spell is cast to clear the way and draw money towards you.

The wealth spell is Dr Omar's general money spell. It is cast to increase your income, bring unexpected money and help you hold on to what you earn. Clients use it when they feel stuck at the same level for years, or when every month ends in shortage no matter how hard they work.

A business success spell is cast on your business itself — its name, its premises or its online store. A luck spell removes bad luck and brings good fortune into your daily life: better chances, open doors and things falling into place. Some clients ask for luck with a specific goal — an interview, a contract, a deal, a competition. Dr Omar casts the luck spell with that goal in mind.

Simple Money Spells to Try at Home

These traditional rituals are easy to do at home and are a good way to begin working with money energy:

Cinnamon money spell

On the first day of the month, blow a pinch of ground cinnamon across your front door from outside to inside while saying: "Money come in, prosperity stay." Leave it until the next day, then sweep it inward.

Bay leaf money spell

Write the amount you need on a dry bay leaf with a pen. Hold it, picture the money arriving, and keep it in your wallet until the money comes. Then burn the leaf safely and give thanks.

Green candle money spell

Carve your name and the word "wealth" into a green candle. Rub it with a little olive oil and cinnamon, light it on a Thursday, and let it burn while you focus on the money flowing to you. Never leave a burning candle unattended.

Home rituals are a good start. For a business, a large debt or a long run of bad luck, a money spell cast by Dr Omar carries much more strength.
