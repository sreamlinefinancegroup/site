Who Is Dr Sheikh Omar Rashid?

Dr Sheikh Omar Rashid is a traditional spiritual healer, psychic and renowned love spell caster who works with clients worldwide through an online service. He casts return ex lover spells, get your ex back spells, stop divorce and marriage spells, voodoo, binding and obsession love spells, and money spells, and he gives psychic readings on love and relationships.

Dr Omar's work is rooted in traditional spiritual healing. He treats every case as personal: a real person, a real relationship and a real problem that needs the right answer — not a one-size-fits-all spell.

His Approach

Every case begins with a reading. Before recommending any spell, Dr Omar looks at what happened between the two people, what the other person is feeling now, whether anyone else is involved, and what is blocking the relationship or the money. Only then does he choose the spell, its materials and its strength.

He is direct with clients. If a spell is the right step, he explains what it will do and how long it usually takes. If something else is needed first — a cleansing, time, or a change in how the client is behaving — he says so.

The fee depends on the spell and the difficulty of the case. Dr Omar quotes one fee after the consultation, and it is agreed before any casting begins. There are no hidden "extra materials" charges added afterwards.
