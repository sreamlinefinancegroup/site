What Is a Love Spell?

A love spell is a ritual cast to create, strengthen or bring back love between two people by working on their emotional and spiritual connection. Dr Sheikh Omar Rashid casts love spells for many purposes: to return an ex lover, to deepen a present relationship, to make someone fall in love, to bring commitment or marriage, and to protect a relationship from interference.

Different love spells work in different ways. Some are gentle and slow, like a sweetening spell that softens a partner over weeks. Others are fast and intense, like a voodoo love spell or an obsession spell. Choosing the right one is the most important part of the work — which is why Dr Omar begins every case with a reading.

Voodoo Love Spell to Get Your Ex Back

The voodoo love spell is one of the strongest traditional love spells. It works through a personal link to the person you love — their name, date of birth, a photograph, and sometimes an item they owned or touched. Through that link the spell reaches their emotions directly, bringing you into their thoughts and pulling them back towards you.

Dr Omar uses the voodoo love spell to get an ex back when a simple return lover spell may not be strong enough: when the breakup was bitter, when your ex has been cold for a long time, or when someone else is influencing them. It is also used to make a present partner more devoted and attentive.

Binding Love Spell

A binding love spell ties two people together so the relationship becomes stable, loyal and lasting. It is used by couples who are already together and want to stay together — to stop cheating, stop wandering eyes and protect the bond from anyone who wants to come between them. Married couples use a binding spell to strengthen their vows; unmarried couples use it to move towards commitment.

Because a binding spell is designed to last, Dr Omar only casts it after a reading confirms that it fits the relationship.

Obsession Spell: Make Him Obsessively Think About Me

An obsession spell — sometimes called a fixation spell — makes a person think about you constantly. Clients ask for a spell to make him obsessively think about me or a spell to make ex crazy in love with you when they want their partner or ex to feel an intense, hard-to-ignore pull. Your image comes to their mind throughout the day; they feel restless when they are not in contact with you.

Dr Omar adjusts the strength of the obsession spell to the situation. For most clients, a strong attraction is what they want — not a spell that makes the other person anxious. He will advise you on this during the reading.

Spell to Make Him Text You Nonstop

A spell to make him text you focuses on communication. It is often used when a partner has gone quiet, when an ex is ignoring messages, or when a new love interest is slow to reply. After casting, clients usually notice more frequent messages, calls and check-ins. It works well together with a return lover spell or an attraction spell.

Spell to Make Your Ex Dream of You Every Night

Dreams are one of the clearest channels a love spell can use. A spell to make your ex dream of you places you in their dreams, where their defences are down. Many people wake up missing someone they dreamed about and reach out the same day. It is a gentle but effective first step before a full return lover spell.

Attraction Spell: Make Your Crush Notice You

Not every love spell is about an ex. An attraction spell is cast to make someone new notice you, feel drawn to you and want to know you better. Clients use it for a crush, a colleague, a friend they have feelings for, or simply to bring a new partner into their life. Dr Omar can cast it on a named person or as a general spell to attract the right love.

Marriage and Commitment Spells

If you are in a relationship that is not moving forward, a commitment spell or marriage spell helps your partner see a future with you. It is used when a partner avoids talking about marriage, delays a proposal or will not introduce you to their family. For couples already married but struggling, see the stop divorce and marriage healing spells.

Break-Up Spell: Spell to Break Up Ex and New Girlfriend

When the person you love is with someone else, a break-up spell is cast to create distance, arguments and doubt in that relationship. Dr Omar usually casts it together with a return lover spell, so that as the new relationship weakens, your ex's attention turns back to you. Read more in the return ex lover spell guide.

Hoodoo, Wiccan and African Traditional Love Spells

People searching for love spells find many traditions. Hoodoo love work uses honey jars, petition papers, roots and oils. Wiccan love spells use candles, crystals, moon phases and herbs such as rose, lavender and cinnamon. African traditional love spells work with ancestral and spiritual forces and are known for their strength. Dr Omar draws on traditional spiritual healing and chooses materials and methods to fit each client's case. You can see examples of common home rituals in the honey jar and candle rituals guide.

How to Choose the Right Love Spell

  • Your ex left and you want them back: return ex lover spell, with a make-them-miss-you element.
  • Your ex is with someone new: return lover spell plus break-up spell.
  • Your ex is cold or angry: voodoo love spell or obsession spell.
  • Your partner is drifting or cheating: binding love spell.
  • Your partner will not commit: commitment or marriage spell.
  • You want someone new to notice you: attraction spell.
  • Your marriage is ending: stop divorce spell.

If you are not sure, simply tell Dr Omar what is happening. He will recommend the spell after reading your situation.

What Happens After a Love Spell Is Cast?

In the days after casting, the person the spell is aimed at usually begins to think of you more often, feel drawn to you and become softer in their words. Contact follows — a message, a call, a visit. Dr Omar gives you instructions for this period and stays in touch as the signs appear. If the signs are slow, he strengthens the spell.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the strongest love spell?

Among traditional love spells, the voodoo love spell and the obsession spell are considered the strongest and fastest. Dr Omar chooses the strength of each spell after reading the client's situation.

Does a voodoo love spell work on an ex?

Yes. The voodoo love spell to get an ex back is one of the spells Dr Omar uses most often when a breakup was bitter or an ex has been cold for a long time.

What is the difference between a binding spell and an obsession spell?

A binding spell makes a relationship loyal, stable and lasting. An obsession spell creates intense attraction and constant thoughts of you. Many clients use an obsession spell first and a binding spell later.

Can a love spell make someone fall in love with me?

An attraction spell is cast to make a named person notice you, feel drawn to you and want to be close to you. Dr Omar can also cast a general spell to attract the right partner.

Are there side effects of love spells?

The most common effects are strong emotions — the person thinks about you more, dreams of you and feels restless when apart. Dr Omar adjusts the strength of each spell so the result is love and attraction, not distress.

Can a love spell be reversed?

Yes. Dr Omar can reverse or release a spell he has cast if the client asks. He can also remove spells cast by others.

How long does a love spell last?

Binding and unconditional love spells are cast to last long-term. Attraction and obsession spells are strongest in the first weeks and are often followed by a binding spell to keep the relationship stable.

Do I need a photo for a love spell?

A photo is helpful but not required. The name and date of birth are enough to create the link.
